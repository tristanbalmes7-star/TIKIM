Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Dack's Catering Services collects customer information necessary to prepare quotations, reserve event dates, coordinate culinary menus, and track booking status through our TIKIM information system. This information includes celebrant names, contact phone numbers, delivery or venue addresses, and guest count specifications.

2. Use of Your Information

Your personal details are used solely to facilitate your event planning, send booking SMS notifications, verify payment proofs, and deliver catering equipment and service staff on your celebration day. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security & Storage

Quotations and draft layouts are stored locally within your browser and on secure servers. Payment proof images uploaded via the Track Booking portal are accessible only by authorized Dack's Catering event managers for account verification.

4. Inquiries & Contact

For inquiries regarding your data or to request deletion of your booking records, contact our management team at Cruz Street, San Ildefonso, Bulacan, or call 0977 752 3458.
